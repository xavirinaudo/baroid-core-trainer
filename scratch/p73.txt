Solids Control 
A solids control operation is a cost effective strategy of maximizing drilling efficiency by separating & removing 
drilled solids from the drilling fluid generated during the drilling process.  
 Mechanical separation of drilled solids is the most effective and economically viable option  
 Solids removal is essential to the optimization of fluid performance 
 Considered a Proactive / Preventive approach to mitigate solid related issues  
 Reduces volumes of waste generated and lowers required dilution volumes. 
 
Implementing successful Solids Control operations requires all the following aspects to be included in an 
engineered solution:
• Planning  
• Design 
• Preparation  
• Installation 
• Operation 
• Data Collection 
• Effective reporting  
• Performance monitoring 
• Continual Improvement 
 
Solids Contamination: A Costly problem  
 Drill solids contamination is unavoidable and can result in devastating consequences. 
 Issues attributed to solids are often not immediately recognized 
 Characteristics of the drilled solids dictate the severity of the contamination and its effects. 
 Fluid treatment costs typically attribute the largest portion of the overall expense to remedy adverse 
effects produced by solids.
